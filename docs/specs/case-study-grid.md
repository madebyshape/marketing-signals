# Case Study Grid

Spec for the Case Study Grid on the Case Studies page: a Category Filter of Filter Buttons above a grid of Case Study Cards, eight to a page in a fixed rhythm of Large and Half Card Sizes, with Pagination beneath. The grid, its filter and its pages are one Sprig component, so choosing a Category or a page swaps the three together without a full load, and the same state is reachable by URL. The Case Study Card is extracted from the Case Study Carousel into a component of its own that both the carousel and the grid render, gaining the Zoom the Blog Cards already have. The Seed command learns to write Categories fields so the page can be reviewed against the design with real volume.

Design: Figma node `9903-14505` in the Marketing Signals file, 1600 wide, named "Group 46352". The node draws the desktop state once at 1520 by 3202: the Category Filter with All Work active, one page of eight Cards, the Pagination on page one of five, and a "View Case Study" Cursor Label at rest over the first Large Card. No hover-free frame, no filtered state, no arrow design for the Category Filter, no tablet frame and no mobile frame exist, so the filter's arrows and fades, the hover states, the tablet and mobile geometry and the mobile card shapes below are decisions, not measurements.

Branch: feature/case-study-grid

Related: the Carousel - Case Study spec, whose inline Slide markup becomes the Case Study Card component and whose Block is refactored onto it; the Carousel - Blog spec, whose Zoom the Card adopts; the Content Seeding spec, whose command gains a Categories field type and whose Out of Scope loses the word "categories"; the FAQ Accordion spec, the only Sprig precedent, whose attribute conventions this follows. ADR-0001 does not apply: the grid is in flow beneath the Hero. ADR-0002 applies: every Case Study the review needs arrives by Seed. ADR-0003 applies and constrains: the Case Studies page is the one `entryCaseStudyListing` page, found by type, so the grid lives in that page template rather than in a Block. ADR-0004 is untouched: the page's Hero still sets the Header Colour. No new ADR: the URL-state rule and the position-derived Card Size are each easy to reverse and are recorded under Further Notes. Vocabulary: `CONTEXT.md`, new "Case Study Grid" section, which gained Case Study Grid, Card Size, Category Filter, Filter Button, All Work, Pagination, Page Number and Page Window during the grilling session; the "Case Study Carousel" section gained Case Study Card, with Case Study Slide narrowed to a Card shown as a Slide, and Category Badge, Zoom and Cursor Label widened to cover the Card.

The branch is code-reviewed against this spec before merge.

## Problem Statement

The Case Studies page exists, is the page the Breadcrumb points to, carries a Hero and Blocks, and shows no Case Studies. Its control panel tab promises editors that "Case Study Entries will automatically pull through here" and nothing does. The Case Studies themselves have a Thumbnail, a Logo, a Tag Line and a Category, and the only place a visitor meets them together is the Case Study Carousel on the Home page, which shows the six an editor picked and cannot be filtered or paged. A visitor who wants the agency's search work, or its PR work, has no way to ask for it. The card markup that would show a Case Study is written once, inline in the carousel Block, so a second surface would have to copy it. Nothing on the site pages through results: the FAQ's Load More appends, and no template paginates. Six Case Studies exist and they are three clients entered twice; six Categories exist, one of them twice, and only two are used. The Seed command that builds review content cannot set a Categories field, so it cannot create the content this page needs to be reviewed at all.

## Solution

The Case Studies page renders the Case Study Grid between its Hero and its Blocks. At the top, the Category Filter: All Work first, then one Filter Button per Category that at least one live Case Study belongs to, in the Category group's order, each a radar dot beside the Category's name in an outlined pill, the active one outlined in the primary purple. Below a thin creme rule, the grid: eight Case Study Cards to a page in the order Large, Half, Half, Half, Half, Large, Half, Half, a Large Card spanning the full width with its Tag Line at 55px semibold, a Half Card taking six of the twelve columns with its Tag Line at 30px medium, every Card the Thumbnail darkened behind its Category Badge, its Logo and its Tag Line with the Highlight in fluro, the whole Card one link with a "View Case Study" Cursor Label and a gentle Zoom on hover. Beneath, centred, the Pagination: a Previous pill, up to five Page Numbers with the current one filled in lilac, and a Next pill in lilac, Previous and Next dimmed at the ends. Pressing a Filter Button swaps the grid to that Category's first page and dims the grid while the request runs; pressing a Page Number or Previous or Next swaps to that page and scrolls the Category Filter to the top of the viewport beneath the Header. Every swap pushes the Category and page into the URL's query string, so the browser's back button, a shared link and a direct load all land on the same state. Below `md` the Category Filter becomes one horizontally scrolling row under a pair of arrows that appear only when there is more to scroll, and the grid becomes one column of 4:3 Cards. The Case Study Card becomes a component the carousel Block also renders. The Seed command gains a Categories field type and thirty-four Case Studies are seeded across the six Categories so five pages exist to review.

## Implementation Decisions

**Page template.** The Case Study Listing page template renders the Hero, then the Case Study Grid, then the Blocks. The grid is wrapped in the section component with `paddingY` at its `base` value and horizontal padding at the site margins, so it takes the page's vertical rhythm without an editor setting. There is no Block and no new field: the page's control panel Tip already says Case Studies pull through automatically, and ADR-0003 fixes the page to one instance found by its entry type. The entry type's field layout is untouched.

**The Sprig component.** One Sprig component template under the Sprig templates path holds the Category Filter, the grid and the Pagination, so one request re-renders all three. It takes two scalar params, `category`, a Category slug or empty for All Work, and `page`, an integer defaulting to one, each defaulted with the null-coalescing pattern the FAQ component set. The page template calls it with the two values read from the request's query string, so a direct load of `?category=organic-search&page=2` renders that state server-side and the swap produces byte-identical markup. Every trigger carries the `sprig` attribute, sets the two values, points its indicator at the grid, and pushes the resulting query string to the address bar with Sprig's push-url attribute, so the back button walks the history and a shared link reproduces the state. The Sprig attribute strings are built as an array and joined, as the FAQ component does, and none contains a `>`.

**Query.** Enabled Case Studies from the Case Study section in structure order, which is the section's default, related to the chosen Category when one is given, paginated by Sprig's paginate helper at eight per page. A `page` beyond the last page is clamped to the last; a `category` that matches no Category is treated as All Work. The Filter Buttons come from the Case Study Category group in its own order, keeping only Categories that at least one enabled Case Study is related to, so a Category with no live work never renders a button. A Case Study with several Categories matches each of them; its Category Badge shows the first, as the carousel already does.

**Category Filter.** A new `filterButton` component renders one Filter Button: a hidden radio input followed by its label, the label a 41px pill, 10px vertical and 15px left and 20px right padding, 8px gap, a 21px radar dot then the Category's name in the base size, medium weight. The radio's `name` is `category`, its value the Category slug or empty for All Work, and the checked one is the active Category. Three looks via an options map: `base` is a Creme 300 outline with the unlit dot, `active` is a Primary outline with the lit dot, applied through the peer-checked pattern, and hover on fine pointers is the Primary outline. The radar dots are the two SVG assets exported from the node, committed to the SVG templates folder and included inline so the lit one takes its colour from the token. The Filter Buttons sit in a row with 10px between them inside a group with a fieldset and legend reading "Filter by category", visually hidden. Beneath the row, 40px down, a 1px Creme 300 rule spanning the site margins, then 40px to the grid.

**Category Filter as a carousel.** Below `md`, and at any width where the row overflows, the row is a Swiper carousel through the existing carousel component with the Filter Buttons as its slides, free mode on, slides sized to their content, 10px between them, and overflow watching on so a row that fits locks and hides its arrows. Two arrow buttons through the shape-button component, `creme-300-outline`, sit over the row's ends and are hidden with Alpine when the row is at its start or end, each backed by a fade from Creme 100 to transparent so the cut-off pill reads as continuing. At 1600 the row is 1289px wide and fits, so the arrows never show there; they appear below roughly 1400px. The arrows and fades are decisions: the node draws none, and this follows the Hello Mustard case studies page the client pointed at.

**Grid and Card Size.** The grid is twelve columns with the house 20px gap. Card Size is derived from position on the page, never stored: the first and sixth Cards of every eight are Large and span twelve columns, the rest are Half and span six from `md`. Below `md` every Card spans twelve. So a page of eight reads Large, Half, Half, Half, Half, Large, Half, Half at every width from `md`, and a filtered page follows the same rhythm from its own first Card. The rule lives in the Sprig component as a loop helper on the index, in the manner of Card Scheme and Slot, and is passed to the Card as its `size` param.

**Case Study Card component.** A new `caseStudyCard` component takes the Case Study, a `size` of `half` or `large`, a `sizes` hint for the picture component and a class. It renders the card the carousel Block renders today: one link to the Case Study wrapping the whole card, 20px corners, black background, overflow clipped; the Thumbnail through the picture component at 90% opacity with the focal point honoured and an empty alt; the top and bottom shades; the Category Badge at the top left, 30px in from `lg` and 20px below; the Logo centred and capped at 30% of the width and 20% of the height; the Tag Line as a paragraph through the alternate heading component in white with the fluro Highlight, at the bottom left, 40px in from `lg` and 20px below, stripped to italic, bold and breaks. The `size` option map sets the shape and the Tag Line's type: Half is the carousel's 16x10 from `md` and 3x4 below with the Tag Line 2xl below `lg` and 3xl from `lg`, medium; Large is 2x1 from `md` and 4x3 below with the Tag Line 3xl below `lg` and 6xl (55px) from `lg`, semibold, with the 6xl token's 0.97 leading and tighter tracking. A new `2x1` named transform is added for the Large step and `4x3` already exists. The carousel Block is refactored to render this component at `half` with its existing 85vw and 47vw sizes hints and loses its inline card classes; it must render pixel for pixel as before.

**Hover, Zoom and the Cursor Label.** On fine pointers entering a Card raises the Cursor Label through the cursor component's window event with "View Case Study", leaving lowers it, exactly as the carousel does today. The Card gains the Zoom: the Thumbnail scales to 1.05 over 600ms with an ease-out inside the clipped frame on hover, using the same classes as the Blog Card so the site has one Zoom, and does not move under reduced motion. Because the Card is shared, the carousel Slides gain the Zoom too. On coarse pointers the tap is the link and nothing else is added.

**Pagination.** A new `pagination` component takes the current page, Sprig's page info and the attributes its triggers need, and renders nothing when there is one page. It renders a row, centred, 10px gaps, 70px below the grid: Previous through the button component as a `button` with the arrow-left icon before "Previous"; the Page Window; Next through the button component with the arrow-right icon after "Next". The enabled end button is the button component's `secondary` fill and the disabled one its `creme-300-outline`, which is what the node draws on page one; a disabled end button carries the `disabled` attribute, 30% opacity and no trigger. The Page Window shows at most five Page Numbers: all of them when there are five pages or fewer, otherwise a window of five that starts two before the current page, clamped so it never runs past the first or last page. Each Page Number is a 42px circle in the base size, medium weight: the current one is a `span` filled Secondary with `aria-current` set to page, the others are `button` elements with a Creme 300 outline and a Secondary fill on hover. Every button in the row is a Sprig trigger for its page with the current Category, so a page change never drops the filter. The button component's base pill is 40px tall against the node's 42px; the 2px is accepted rather than adding a size.

**Swap behaviour.** All triggers target the Sprig component's root and replace it whole. During a request the grid sits at 50% opacity with pointer events off through the indicator class, as the FAQ button does. After a swap that changed the page, a Sprig after-swap listener scrolls the Category Filter's top to the top of the viewport beneath the fixed Header, using the header height token so nothing hides under it; a swap that changed the Category does not scroll, since the filter is already in view. A Category change always requests page one. The component's root carries a polite live region so a screen reader hears that the grid changed.

**Accessibility.** The Category Filter is a fieldset of radio inputs with visible labels, so arrow keys move between Categories and the chosen one is announced. The Cards are a list; each link's accessible name is its contents, the Category, the Logo's alt and the Tag Line, with no `aria-label`. The Thumbnail's alt is empty; the Logo's alt is the Case Study's title. The Pagination is a `nav` labelled "Pagination", the current Page Number carries `aria-current`, disabled end buttons are real disabled buttons and leave the tab order. Nothing is read twice.

**Empty states.** A Case Study without a Thumbnail renders its Card on solid black; without a Logo, Tag Line or Category it drops that part and nothing else. One page renders no Pagination. A Category that no enabled Case Study belongs to renders no Filter Button. If a request still lands on an empty page, from a stale link after content was unpublished, the grid renders a single line reading "No case studies found" in the base size and the Pagination renders nothing. A section with no Case Studies at all renders All Work alone over that line.

**Seed command.** The Seed command's value resolver gains a Categories field arm. It takes a list of Category titles, looks each up by title in the field's source group, creates a Category with that title in the group when none exists, and relates the entry to them in the given order. It is one arm modelled on the Entries arm, and the content-seeding spec's Out of Scope line loses "categories". A Seed for a Case Study sets `thumbnail`, `logo`, `tagLine` and `categoriesCaseStudy` on the entry it creates.

**Review content.** Thirty-four Case Studies seeded into the Case Study section, one Seed file each under the scratch folder, run in a loop, so All Work has five pages, the last with two Cards, and every Category has at least one page. The clients cycle through the eight the node draws, Rayban, Better Bathrooms, The Tree Center, Marriott, GXO, Ultimate Performance, Qantas and Missguided, with their Thumbnails and Logos from the node's assets, already downloaded into the Seed folder, and varied Tag Lines with the Highlight on the result. Categories are spread so Organic Search and Pay Per Click each have more than eight Case Studies and page, and the other four have between one and six. The three duplicate `-2` Case Studies and the duplicate "Digital PR" Category (the higher of the two ids) are deleted by hand in the control panel before seeding, since the command does not delete; the three originals are kept and given their Thumbnail, Logo, Tag Line and Category by Seed so they match. The Seeds are not committed.

**Docs.** `CONTEXT.md` gained the Case Study Grid section and the Card terms during the grilling session. The content-seeding spec's Out of Scope is amended. No new ADR.

## Testing Decisions

There is no test suite. Evidence replaces tests: screenshots from the running DDEV site, taken with agent-browser per the evidence doc, compared against the Figma node at the same width.

**Seams.** The primary seam is the Case Studies page URL through the global layout, with the Category and page in its query string: every state the spec names is reachable by a direct load and renders the same markup the swap produces, so no screenshot depends on clicking. The Sprig swaps are proven at the same seam by loading the page and pressing. The Case Study Card is proven twice through its two callers: the grid, and the carousel Block on the Home page, whose after must match its before. The secondary seam is the Seed command's dry-run and real output for a Case Study Seed with Categories. The styleguide has no listing preview and gains nothing.

## Out of Scope

- A search box, a sort control, multiple active Categories or a filter on Industry or Year.
- Load More or infinite scroll; the page paginates.
- Filtering by URL segment, such as `/case-studies/organic-search`; the Category group has no URLs and the state is a query string.
- Changing Blitz's query-string caching. Filtered and paged URLs are served dynamically by the existing settings.
- The Case Study entry page itself and its Hero.
- An editor field for Card Size, or pinning a Case Study to a Large slot.
- A Category Filter on the Blog or any other section, though the Sprig component and the filter, Card and Pagination components are written to allow one.
- Deleting content by Seed; the four duplicates are removed by hand.
- Replacing the review Thumbnails with final photography.

## Further Notes

- The Category and page live in the URL rather than in Sprig state alone so that a filtered grid is a page like any other: shareable, bookmarkable, back-button-able and loadable without script. It is recorded here rather than as an ADR because it is easy to reverse.
- Card Size follows position rather than a field because the design draws a rhythm, not a set of chosen features, and a filtered page should keep it. It is the same rule shape as Card Scheme and Slot.
- Blitz in production caches every URI but has query-string caching off, so `/case-studies` is cached and any `?category=` or `?page=` URL is served dynamically. Development has caching off. Nothing changes.
- The Sprig component is called with the query-string values so the server render and the swap share one code path; there is no client-side state.
- The Large Card is 1520 by 738 in the node, 2.06:1. The standard prefers a common ratio, and 2x1 puts the Card at 760 tall, 22px more than drawn; the nearest alternative, 16x9, would be 855 tall. The Half Card keeps the carousel's 16x10.
- The node's Pagination pills are 42px tall against the button component's 40px, and its Page Numbers are 42px circles; the circles are drawn at 42px and the pills accept the 2px.
- The node names the active Filter Button "Primary Outline" and the Next pill "Primary" but fills the latter with the Secondary token; the button component's naming stands.
- The node's "View Case Study" pill is the Cursor Label at rest over the first Large Card, not a button on the Card; on coarse pointers the tap is the link, as on the carousel.
- The node's tag line styles: the Half Card's 30px medium with -1.2px tracking is the 3xl token with tighter tracking; the Large Card's 55px semibold with 0.97 leading and -2.2px tracking is the 6xl token with tighter tracking.
- Of the eight clients the node draws, the Marriott, Ultimate Performance and Missguided Logos are composite vectors the design context could not export as one file; they are exported from their master components in Figma before seeding, per the nested-instance rule.
- The three existing Case Studies keep their ids and slugs so any link to them survives; only their `-2` duplicates go.
- The design font is "Noi Grotesk Trial"; the site serves Noi Grotesk, so no font change.
